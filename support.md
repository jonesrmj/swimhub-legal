# SwimHub Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email **[support@swimhub.fit](mailto:support@swimhub.fit)**.

## Common questions

### How do I delete my account?

Open the **Account** tab and tap **Delete Account**. If you signed in with
Apple, you will be asked to confirm with Apple first.

This removes your profile, posts, comments, replies, photos, likes, follows,
workouts and journal entries from our servers. A few things are kept or left
behind — for example, workouts and journal entries saved on your iPhone stay
there until you delete the app. The
[privacy policy](privacy.md#deleting-your-account) lists them all.

If deletion fails, or you can no longer sign in, email us from the address on
your account and we will delete it for you.

### I didn't get my verification or password reset email

Check your spam or junk folder first — the email comes from
**noreply@swimhub.fit**. If it isn't there, tap **Resend verification email**
on the sign-in screen. Still nothing? Email us.

### How do I report a post, comment or person?

Tap the **⋯** menu on the post or comment and choose **Report Post** or
**Report Comment**. To report a
person, open their profile and tap **Report**. We review every report.

### How do I stop seeing someone?

Tap the **⋯** menu on their post or comment and choose **Block**, or block them
from their profile. You will no longer see their posts, comments or replies.

### Can I turn off access to Apple Health?

Yes. In the iPhone **Settings** app, go to **Health › Data Access & Devices ›
SwimHub** and turn off access. Workouts you already saved stay in your workout
log.
