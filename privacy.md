# SwimHub Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2 November 2026**

This explains what SwimHub collects, why, where it goes, and what happens to it.

## What we collect

**When you create an account**

- Your name. If you sign in with Apple and choose not to share your name, we
  use "SwimHub User" until you change it.
- Your email address. If you sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email",
  this is the private relay address Apple gives us, not your real address.
- Your date of birth — used to check you are 13 or older before you can post
  publicly, and not shown to anyone else
- A username you choose

**When you use the app**

- Your bio and profile photo, if you add them
- Posts, comments and replies you write
- Photos you attach to posts. Location data is removed from these photos
  before they are uploaded.
- Workouts and journal entries you record
- Who you follow, and who you have blocked
- Likes
- Reports you make about other people's content

**From Apple Health, if you allow it**

See [Apple Health data](#apple-health-data) below.

**Automatically**

- Usage and device information collected by Firebase Analytics — see
  [Analytics](#analytics) below
- Error logs from our servers, which can include your account ID

## Where it goes

These services store or process your data on our behalf:

- **Firebase (Google)** — your account and sign-in, your profile (name, email,
  username, bio), your profile photo, and analytics
- **Amazon Web Services** — your profile, posts, comments, replies, likes,
  follows, blocks, photos you post, workouts, journal entries, reports and error
  logs. Stored in the United States.
- **Apple** — if you sign in with Apple, Apple handles that sign-in
- **OpenAI** — the details you type when you ask SwimHub to generate a workout
  (see [AI-generated workouts](#ai-generated-workouts))
- **Cloudflare** — delivers email you send to support@swimhub.fit

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Apple Health data

If you allow it, SwimHub **reads** your swimming workouts from Apple Health so
you can import workouts recorded on your Apple Watch. It reads each workout's
date, duration, distance, pool length or open-water setting, and the laps and
segments recorded during it. SwimHub does not write anything to Apple Health.

Health data stays on your iPhone unless you choose to save an imported workout.
When you do, the workout you reviewed — its date, duration, distance and sets —
is saved to your workout log and stored with your other workouts. The raw data
from Apple Health is never uploaded.

**We never use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing, never sell it,
and never share it with anyone other than the services above that store your
workout log for you.** It is not sent to OpenAI or used for analytics.

You can turn off SwimHub's access at any time in the iPhone Settings app, under
Health › Data Access & Devices.

## AI-generated workouts

When you ask SwimHub to generate a workout, the focus, stroke, level and any
requests you type are sent to OpenAI to write the workout. We do not send your
name, email, account ID, health data or workout history. OpenAI processes this
under its API terms. We do not store your request.

Don't type personal information into the requests box.

## Analytics

SwimHub uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand how the app is used —
for example, how many people open it each day and which screens they visit.
It collects:

- An identifier for your installation of the app
- Device model, iOS version and app version
- Approximate location (country or region), worked out from your IP address
- Which screens you visit and when you use the app

We do not connect analytics to your SwimHub account, name or email, and we do
not use it for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps and
websites.

## What is private and what is public

**Public** — anyone can see it, with or without a SwimHub account:

- Your profile photo

**Visible to other SwimHub users:**

- Your name, username and bio
- Anything you post to the Community feed, including photos
- A workout or journal entry you attach to a post — including a journal
  entry's mood and thoughts
- Who you follow, who follows you, and your follower and following counts
- Your comments, replies and likes

**Private** — only you can see it:

- Your journal entries and workout log, unless you attach one to a post
- Your date of birth
- Your email address
- Your blocked list

## Moderation

Text you post is checked automatically for offensive language before it is
stored. This happens on our own servers — no outside service sees it. It is an
automated filter and it sometimes gets things wrong — if it refuses something
it should not have, email us.

**Photos are not scanned automatically.** They are reviewed if someone reports
them.

When someone reports content, we keep a copy of the reported text as it was at
the time of the report, even if it is later deleted. This is so we can review
it and act on repeated behaviour. These records are kept for up to 90 days
after a report is resolved.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from the Account screen. This removes your
profile, posts, comments, replies, photos, likes, follows, workouts and journal
entries from our servers — including the image files themselves, not just the
posts that showed them. It then deletes your sign-in account and, if you used
Sign in with Apple, tells Apple to revoke SwimHub's access.

Some things are not removed, and you should know about them:

- **Reports.** If your content was reported, the record of that report may be
  kept for up to 90 days after it was resolved, as described above. Reports
  you made are also kept until they expire. We keep them so a pattern of
  behaviour cannot be erased by deleting an account.
- **Data on your iPhone.** Workouts, journal entries and cached images saved on
  your iPhone stay there until you delete the app.
- **Your sign-in, in one case.** If you signed up with an email and password
  and have not signed in recently, deletion can stop partway: your data is
  removed but your sign-in (email and password) remains. Sign in again and
  delete once more, or email us and we will finish it.
- **Very active accounts.** If you posted a very large amount, some older posts
  or comments may not be removed. Email us and we will remove any that remain.
- **Logs and analytics.** Server error logs, which can include your account ID,
  are not deleted. Analytics data is not linked to your account, so it is not
  deleted either.

## Children

SwimHub is not directed at children under 13, and under-13 accounts cannot post
to the public feed, comment, or reply.

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and
we will delete it.

## Your choices

- **See or correct your information** — the Account screen
- **Delete everything** — the Account screen, or email us
- **Stop sharing Apple Health data** — iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access
  & Devices
- **Stop seeing someone** — block them from their profile or their content

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights over your data.
Email us and we will help.

## Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access controls.
No system is perfectly secure, but we do not store passwords ourselves — sign-in
is handled by Firebase.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way we will let you know in the app.
