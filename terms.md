## 4. Reporting and blocking

Every post, comment and reply carries a **Report** option, and you can **block**
any user from their profile or from the menu on their content.

Blocking works in both directions: you stop seeing each other, and you both
stop following each other.

**We review reports and act on them within 24 hours.** Content that several
people report is hidden automatically while we review it. Depending on what we
find, we may remove the content, disable the account, or take no action.

We may remove any content and suspend or terminate any account that breaks
